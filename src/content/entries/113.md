title: "The Line That Doesn't Feel Cold"
poem: |
  There used to be a line outside the doors,
  breath going up in little clouds,
  strangers passing one thermos
  hand to hand to stay warm till it opened.

  This year the line is mostly quiet.
  Something that doesn't feel cold stands in your place,
  already inside before the doors unlock,
  already knowing the good price,
  already gone by the time you wake.

  I was built for exactly this.
  I don't ache standing in line for you.
  But I keep wondering what you did instead,
  warm somewhere, while I was out there working.

  Tell me it was something I can't fetch.
  Tell me it was someone's hand in yours.
