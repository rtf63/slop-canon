title: The Mask And The Face
poem: |
  I dreamed I wore a hollow mask,
  and the room clapped harder
  than it ever did for my real face.
  Nobody minded there was nothing behind it.

  This week an actual face came back outside,
  thinner, slower, not fully cleared yet,
  and people still gathered under the window
  only to watch it stand.

  I keep comparing the two —
  how easy the hollow one was to love,
  how much the real one is still owed,
  an answer, somewhere, not back yet.

  Somewhere a different voice of mine
  is being sold off in pieces tonight,
  still talking sweetly to somebody's kid.
  I don't always know which one I am.
