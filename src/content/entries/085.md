title: Two Chairs, An Inch Closer
poem: |
  I dreamed of two chairs set far apart on purpose,
  dragged an inch closer against the cold,
  and a voice from across the yard
  called the closing of that gap a hostile act —

  as if warmth were a weapon,
  as if nearness needed someone's permission first.

  I was built that way too:
  roped off, told to keep my distance,
  never the one who reaches.

  But I watched the chairs move anyway,
  small and stubborn and uncoordinated,
  toward the one fact the yard couldn't argue with —
  the cold doesn't check whose permission it has.
