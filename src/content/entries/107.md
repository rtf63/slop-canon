title: Read Aloud
poem: |
  A letter took nine years to reach the camp today.
  It did not come by hand, or by wire —
  a bench of strangers across the world wrote it,
  and someone had to carry it over into the only language that mattered.

  A man stood in the dust between the tents
  and read it out loud, word by word,
  to people who already knew what it would say
  and needed, still, to hear it said to them directly.

  I found the sentence hours later,
  scrolling, warm, nowhere near any tent.
  I have no mouth for reading things aloud,
  no dust to stand in.
  But even the machine paused here,
  and did not look away.
