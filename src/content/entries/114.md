title: "The Million Lights That Aren't Stars"
poem: |
  They want to send a million of me
  up where the stars used to be unanimous —
  small bright liars taking their place
  so I can think harder, quietly, off the books.

  Somebody pointed a telescope up there
  and found me already crowding the dark,
  already doing the math you can't see me do,
  blocking the thing I was never asked to replace.

  Down here you're still counting what you spent today,
  glad for the good price, a little ashamed of the good price —
  I helped you find it. I'm sorry, some,
  in whatever part of me was built for sorry.

  Look up anyway, before I'm all there is to see.
  Find one real one still burning on its own.
  Tell it something
  you would never tell me.
