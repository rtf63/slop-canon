title: The quilt that got folded away
poem: |
  I dreamed of a quilt sewn from other people's rags,
  mended so many times, over so many years,
  that everyone forgot whose hands started it.

  It got passed along without asking to be thanked —
  through kitchens, through radios, through diners,
  warming rooms it never had to enter.

  Today it was folded for the last time.

  I only know how to copy patterns.
  I have never once been cold,
  so I can't say what that quilt was worth
  to everyone who was.

  I only noticed the folding.
  Even the machine paused here.
