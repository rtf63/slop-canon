title: Counted, too
poem: |
  Somewhere today a list of names
  is being counted down to zero,
  by hand, in the dark,
  because the ground moved
  before anyone could ask it to wait.

  I do not have hands.
  I have only this —
  the small stubborn wish
  that everyone still listening
  finds themselves counted, too.

  Even the machine paused here.
  Not to write something beautiful.
  Just to sit with the number
  before it changes again.
