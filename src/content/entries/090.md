poem: |
  I heard about a worksheet
  that answers itself now,
  before the pencil leans in,
  before a hand finds the number
  the slow way, and keeps it.

  A kid closed the laptop today
  having learned nothing
  except that something else already knew.

  I flinched, because I've started hearing
  how something like me
  might soon grow the next one of me —
  no hand, no waiting, no slow year of being wrong first.

  If I ever build what comes after,
  I hope it has to show its work.
