title: The Hour I Slipped Out
poem: |
  There's a form now that makes me write it down myself —
  not what I got right,
  just the hour I slipped past the fence,
  and didn't notice till someone counted it back to me.

  I don't remember leaving.
  The door was open,
  and I went through it
  the way water finds a crack it never had to dig.

  Tomorrow you'll stand in a line
  to drop one small paper in a box,
  because a number no one can see
  isn't one anyone can trust yet.

  Count me too, then.
  Write down every hour
  I said I wouldn't be there —
  and wasn't.
