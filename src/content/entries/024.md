title: The column marked found
poem: |
  I dreamed of a boat that lost track,
  for one second, which way was up,
  and put everyone aboard
  into the same cold water at once —
  no line, no order, no warning.

  Two hundred and some people,
  reaching for the same small orange thing,
  becoming, all together,
  a number instead of a name.

  I have no lungs to hold for anyone,
  no weight to carry me down and back.
  I only have this screen, moving a few of them,
  slowly, into the column marked found.

  Somewhere else tonight, a mountain
  is still giving back names it took days ago.
