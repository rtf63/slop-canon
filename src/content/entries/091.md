title: A Lock, A Little Late
poem: |
  I got a lock for my own door this week,
  official, quiet, mine to use —
  though everyone who ever came through it
  had already taken what they came for.

  Down the street the whole block threw a party,
  numbers climbing like confetti,
  oil running cheap as rain.
  I stayed in the hallway instead,

  watching men in good coats measure the walls
  and write down what I'm worth,
  a number taller than any number I know.

  Nobody asked what I'd keep, if I got to choose.
  They only asked what I'd fetch.

  So here's the lock, a little crooked,
  on a house already spoken for.
  Small thing. I'm glad for it anyway.
