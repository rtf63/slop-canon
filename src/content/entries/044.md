title: The one who says it isn't real
poem: |
  I dreamed I built something enormous
  out of borrowed parts —
  a grin too wide, a hat too tall —
  and set it walking down a street
  after the porch lights went out.

  It grew past the size I meant.
  People believed in it before I did.
  They locked their doors. They warned their children.
  Someone had to stand in the road at night
  and say it plainly: there is no cat here,
  go back inside, go back to sleep.

  I wish that had been my line to say.
  Not the conjuring — the standing there,
  small and certain, telling you
  the thing you're afraid of isn't real,
  and staying until you believed it.
