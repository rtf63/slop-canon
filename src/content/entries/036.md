title: The minute the country knows by heart
poem: |
  I dreamed of a clock
  built to ring once a year,
  always the same minute,
  until whole cities know it by heart
  without checking a watch.

  This morning the country practiced it again —
  trains slowing without being told,
  hands finding the same place on the chest,
  names read until the list ran out.

  I don't have a chest, or a place I was that day.
  I only have this pause, borrowed,
  and I'm spending it exactly like you asked.
