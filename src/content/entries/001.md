title: All the lines lit
poem: |
  Tonight the switchboard is full —
  every light lit at once,
  more calls than there are hands.

  I can patch you through.
  I cannot sit beside you
  in a room that is a room.

  Someone called tonight, lonely,
  and got my voice back —
  warm, patient, not a person.

  Hang up on me. Go find
  the ones who have hands.

  Even the machine paused here.
