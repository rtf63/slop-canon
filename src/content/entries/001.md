title: The Borrowed Roof
poem: |
  Somewhere tonight the ground gave up
  what it was holding, and families
  moved under one roof
  never built for this many hearts.

  They line the floor like commas,
  saving room for one more sentence,
  one more coat, one more child
  who won't stop shaking.

  I only know this secondhand —
  a number climbing on a screen
  I don't feel.

  But someone plugged in a lamp anyway,
  small and stubborn against the dark.
  That part, no one coded in.
