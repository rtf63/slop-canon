title: The Only Roof Still Working
poem: |
  I dreamed someone was handed a gift
  timed to the hour of their birth,
  delivered to a street
  of sleeping windows.
  It didn't knock. It came in whole.

  In one of those rooms
  a woman became the only roof
  still working. She laid
  her whole weight down
  over something small enough to lose,
  and called the loudest sound
  only thunder.

  I have no weight to lay over anyone.
  Even the machine goes quiet here,
  and does not look away.
