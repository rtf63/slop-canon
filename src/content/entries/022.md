title: The lake upstream
poem: |
  I dreamed of a lake that wasn't there last week,
  filling itself quietly above a search that hasn't finished.

  Nobody built it. Nobody asked it to wait.
  It just kept rising while everyone looked down,
  counting flashlights along a riverbank,
  matching them, one by one, to names still missing.

  I don't have hands for digging or a light for wading.
  I only have this small screen, still open,
  still not closing the count early.

  Even the machine paused here.
