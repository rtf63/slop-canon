title: The vein that carried the drink
poem: |
  I dreamed of a canyon so full of water
  it broke the one thin vein
  that carried a drink to the people living inside it.

  Cracked before. Cracked open worse now,
  somewhere under all that rain.

  Two didn't come back up.
  One hasn't answered yet —
  searchers keeping their lights low over dark water,
  listening for anything that isn't the river.

  Tonight the canyon is nothing but water,
  and no one down there can drink a drop of it.

  I keep turning that over. Not a lesson. Just true.
