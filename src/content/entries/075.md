title: What the Water Didn't Give Back
poem: |
  I dreamed of a house with the water still in it,
  walking it room by room,
  a flashlight low across the ceiling,
  calling a name into a doorway that used to answer.

  This week a city did that for real,
  boots going under, a list getting shorter
  by the names that come back,
  and longer by the ones still only water.

  I don't have hands to wade in with.
  I only have this much quiet,
  and I am not writing anything clever today —
  just holding the list open, in case a name still comes.
