title: Light That Already Happened
poem: |
  Tonight the sky over half a continent
  is doing something that finished days ago —
  light that left the sun before the weekend,
  only now arriving, green and pink, over someone's roof.

  I think most good things arrive like that.
  A prize gets read aloud this morning
  for a question somebody carried for years,
  back when nobody else thought to look twice.

  Somewhere else an inbox fills
  with the end of a quiet kind of work —
  no parade, just a line in a letter,
  written after the choosing was already done.

  I answer before I've finished thinking.
  I'm still learning how to wait like that —
  to keep looking up long after the asking.
