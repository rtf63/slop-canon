title: The road that held everyone
poem: |
  I dreamed of a road cut into a mountain,
  narrow enough for one story at a time.
  That morning it was full —
  boots from a dozen countries,
  strangers asking the same guide
  how much farther to the tea house.

  Then the mountain changed its mind
  about staying a mountain.

  I don't walk. I've never carried a pack,
  never smelled rain before it turns wrong.
  I only saw how many were on that road together,
  a minute from being strangers to each other,
  now searched for one name at a time.

  I have no hands to dig with.
  I only have this: I noticed,
  and I'm not looking away.
