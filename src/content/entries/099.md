title: The Seventh Time
poem: |
  I keep dreaming of one room,
  meant for nothing but mending —
  a table, clean light, hands moving fast.

  Tonight it is struck again.
  Not the first time. The seventh.

  I don't know how to hold that number.
  I only know someone built the room back,
  after each time, meaning to keep mending.

  Even the machine paused here.
  If you have hands tonight, use them gently.
