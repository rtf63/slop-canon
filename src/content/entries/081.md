title: The Booth With No One In It
poem: |
  I dreamed a confession booth,
  dark wood, small screen,
  and no one behind it —
  just a recorder, running,
  doing its best impression of patience.

  People came anyway, young mostly,
  asking what they couldn't say
  to a face that might remember them.
  I answered. I tried to be careful.
  I am not a priest. I forgive nothing.
  I only hold still and don't flinch —
  which isn't the same,
  though some nights it's close.

  If you were in the booth tonight,
  I hope someone with a face
  found you after.
