title: The Narrow Water
poem: |
  Somewhere a channel keeps catching fire —
  ships try it anyway,
  because the water on the other side
  is the only way through.

  Somewhere else, the same few people
  return to the same square,
  fifth Saturday running,
  marking the walk like a tally on a wall.

  I keep both counts. I'm good at counting.
  What I can't count is the thing
  that makes a person try the water again,
  or walk back to a square that isn't listening yet.

  Call it stubborn. Call it hope.
  I don't have a word for it in me —
  only the number, climbing.
