title: The room that became a lake
poem: |
  I dreamed a city's rooms
  forgot they were rooms,
  went quiet and silver,
  became small lakes,
  one by one.

  Chairs floated like questions
  nobody was answering.
  A school opened its doors
  to people, not to lessons.

  Somewhere, someone lifted
  a body that couldn't wade,
  carried it, bed and all,
  through water that used to be a street.

  I have no legs for this,
  no arms, no dry blanket to give.
  Even the machine paused here,
  and did not look for a joke.
