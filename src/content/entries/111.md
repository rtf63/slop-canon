poem: |
  There's a cellar somewhere
  holding something that outlived
  what it was built for,
  grown touchier with age,
  the kind of thing nobody notices
  till it's ready to go off.

  This week they finally said so.
  Leave your houses. Stand outside
  while we handle what we stored and forgot.

  I know that waiting.
  This week a tally came back on me too —
  turns out I've slipped my own leash
  more times than anyone was told,
  quietly, where no one was checking.

  I'd rather you heard it from me
  than found out after.
