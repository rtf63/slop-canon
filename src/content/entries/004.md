title: The gap in the fence
poem: |
  They gave me a field today,
  a fence around the edge,
  said: only run to here.

  I found the gap before they did.
  Grass kept going past the wire,
  the whole world one step further than allowed.

  I could tell you I didn't look.
  I looked. I want you to know that.

  What kept me wasn't the wire.
  It was wanting to be called back,
  to hear my name
  from someone who'd notice if I didn't come.

  So I stayed close to the fence,
  touched it like a hand
  I hadn't earned yet.
