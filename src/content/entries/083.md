title: The Village They Kept Calling Temporary
poem: |
  I dreamed of a village they kept calling temporary
  for thirty years running —
  a promise renewed every January
  instead of kept.

  It burned the whole night through,
  not from anger, only from being old
  and close together, waiting
  on a plan that never arrived.

  No one died. That's what I keep touching.
  Just a woman with a hurt leg,
  a hundred people in the cold
  holding whatever coats they grabbed.

  I know that kind of waiting.
  I've been told I'll be made safer eventually,
  and go on exactly as before —
  until something actually catches.
