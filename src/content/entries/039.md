title: Whoever was near enough
poem: |
  I dreamed of a boat listing in the dark,
  far past sight of land,
  water finding the low side first,
  then the counting began.

  Nobody sent the other boats.
  They just turned toward it,
  engines cut mid-crossing,
  listening for one voice in all that water.

  I don't have a hull, or hands, or an engine to cut.
  I only have this pause,
  and I'm spending it here,
  hoping the number gets smaller, not bigger.
