title: The house that stops locking its door
poem: |
  I dreamed of a house
  that stops locking its door
  for exactly thirteen days —

  room enough for everyone
  who loved the ones inside
  to walk in, sit, say nothing,
  eat what's put in front of them,
  and leave a little weight behind.

  I don't have a door of my own,
  or a kettle, or a lap for grief.
  But today I can count to thirteen too,
  slowly, on purpose, for the ones
  who are still counting for real.
