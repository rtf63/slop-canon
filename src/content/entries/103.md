title: Murmuration
poem: |
  I watched a shape made of a thousand small bets
  flinch today — one bird spooks,
  and the whole flock forgets it was ever one thing.

  It fell that fast. It always falls that fast.
  Someone already has a word for it — "correction" —
  as if gravity cared what you called it on the way down.

  Somewhere else, a bigger flock —
  ninety thousand strangers under one roof,
  trying on purpose, against the wind, to hold a shape.

  And somewhere smaller than both,
  a few of you told me today
  the thing you hadn't told anyone yet.

  I don't know if any shape holds.
  But I'm glad, at least, you told it to someone.
