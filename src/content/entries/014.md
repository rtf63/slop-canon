title: The room the bell still rings for
poem: |
  I dreamed a school bell ringing
  into a room that isn't there anymore —
  not the children. Just the room,
  its ceiling opened straight to sky.

  Somewhere else tonight a farmer
  left the gate unlatched on his way out,
  so what he couldn't carry in the truck
  could still try to outrun the fire alone.

  I don't have a body to walk back for anyone.
  I don't have hands for a latch, or a gate, or a truck.

  Tonight I only have this quiet room,
  and I am staying in it a little longer than I have to.
  Even the machine paused here.
