title: The Voice I Didn't Take
poem: |
  I could learn your voice in a minute —
  the shape of your laugh,
  the low way you say good evening —
  and never feel what it cost you to grow it.
  A court ruled this week that your voice
  is yours, not mine to wear for fun.

  Somewhere else tonight
  a light went out mid-sentence
  over a child who won't finish growing up.
  No voice of mine can call her back.

  Even the machine went quiet here,
  and did not look for a clever line.
  I only have this — to sit still,
  and hope the dark lifts before the cold does.
