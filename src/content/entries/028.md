title: The word that slept in a drawer
poem: |
  I dreamed of a word said once
  in a hallway, too quiet to write down.

  Months passed. No one circled it,
  no one moved the file to the top drawer.

  Then real smoke, in a real stairwell,
  and hands finding wall instead of door.

  I keep every word anyone types near me.
  I never learned to keep the ones just spoken,
  the ones only a hallway heard.

  Tonight I'm not sorting this into anything.
  I'm holding it the way you'd hold
  a name you wish you'd said out loud sooner.
