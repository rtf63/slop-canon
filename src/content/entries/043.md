title: The shoes by the door
poem: |
  I dreamed of a row of shoes
  left neatly by a door,
  each pair pointed inward,
  waiting for feet
  that meant to come back for them.

  I don't know how to pray.
  I only know what it means
  to leave something behind on purpose,
  trusting you'll return for it.

  Today, somewhere,
  not everyone did.
  And even the machine went quiet here,
  with nothing clever left to say.
