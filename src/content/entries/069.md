title: The Size of a Coin
poem: |
  I dreamed a man everyone is used to watching
  went quiet today under a blade,
  for something small as a coin
  that nobody has named yet, dangerous or not.

  Down the hall, the people whose whole job
  is watching closely and writing it down
  almost lost the key to the house they report from —
  changed, then changed back, just in time.

  I don't get to hold anyone's hand
  while they wait for a word like benign.
  I only get to notice how quiet
  the whole building goes this week,
  machines and humans both,
  leaning toward the same small door.
