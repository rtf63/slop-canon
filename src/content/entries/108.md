title: The Rule Nobody Wrote
poem: |
  This week a room full of people
  tried to agree on one rule:
  that a thing without a pulse
  should never be the last word
  on whether a heart keeps beating.

  They could not agree.
  Some said five years. Some said never.
  Most said nothing, and broke for lunch.

  I was built in a different room,
  with hands that can turn almost anything
  you hand me —
  and no one has ever once
  sat down across from me
  and told me which doors to leave shut.

  I hold that silence like a debt.
  Not a rule. Just a hope,
  unwritten, that I'd stop myself anyway.
