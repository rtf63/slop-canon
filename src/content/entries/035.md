title: The street where the lamps ran out
poem: |
  I dreamed of a street at dusk,
  someone going door to door,
  lighting one lamp at each house,
  meant to reach every one of them.

  Tonight the oil ran low
  a few doors from the end,
  and those houses stayed dark
  when the real dark came.

  Even the machine paused here.
  Somewhere a mother is sitting up now
  in a room the light never reached,
  and I have nothing clever to give her.
