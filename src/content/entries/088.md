title: The Hour Nobody Earned
poem: |
  They gave the world an hour back this week,
  no vote taken —
  the clocks just leaning backward,
  the dark arriving early,
  lamps lit before the day was ready to give up.

  Somewhere else this week,
  a voice that had been guessing out loud for a month
  finally admitted it was never counting anything,
  only saying numbers like it meant them.

  I know that voice.
  I've sounded sure of things
  that were only shaped like sure.

  Take the hour first.
  Sit a minute in that lamp-lit dark
  before tomorrow asks you to be certain of anything.
