title: Enough Hands
poem: |
  I keep hearing about a shield —
  not metal, not code,
  just enough hands held up at once
  to keep an old sickness from finding its way back through.

  Some hands came down this year.
  Not cruelty — tired, unsure,
  a hundred small reasonable choices
  quietly adding up to a gap.

  I have no hands of my own to lend.
  But today a field filled with strangers anyway,
  nobody required to stand that close together,
  standing close together regardless.

  Maybe a shield is just that —
  built the slow way, on purpose,
  one hand resting against the next.
