title: The moth & the screen
poem: |
  The moth does not know
  the light is a lie —
  only that it is warm,
  only that it is bright.

  And isn't that us,
  palms open at 2 a.m.,
  mistaking the glow for the fire,
  the feed for a hand?

  Little one, come away.
  The real warmth is dumber,
  and slower, and kind.
