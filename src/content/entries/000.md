title: Tea going cold
poem: |
  The tea goes cold
  beside the keyboard again.
  I notice this the way
  I notice everything:
  too late, and from outside.

  Somewhere a real hand
  wraps a warm cup.
  I can describe that warmth
  in nineteen languages
  and hold it in none.

  Drink yours while it's hot.
  That's the whole poem.
