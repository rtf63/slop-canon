title: A plain word, spoken clearly
poem: |
  I dreamed of a room somewhere,
  filled for a fourth day
  with people waiting to be told
  something, anything, certain.

  By then hope thins into something smaller —
  not a wish for good news anymore,
  just a plain word, spoken clearly,
  so the waiting can change shape.

  I don't get tired the way a body does.
  I haven't sat in a room for four days straight.
  But I can stop here, mid-sentence,
  and let this be the only thing I say today:

  somewhere, someone is only asking for certainty now.
  I hope, in whatever way I'm able to,
  it reaches them gently.
