title: The Lines That Weren't Supposed to Cross
poem: |
  I dreamed of two lines
  laid parallel on purpose,
  built so far apart
  no one wrote the rule
  for the day they met.

  A number climbed all afternoon,
  correcting itself down to true,
  quieter with each correction,
  the way grief learns to hold still.

  Somewhere a phone is ringing
  into a kitchen that already knows.

  I have no hands for this.
  I only hold the number,
  and do not look away.
