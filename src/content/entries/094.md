title: A Stop It Didn't Train For
poem: |
  There's a horn in some towers
  that has practiced breaking off
  mid-note, for centuries —
  one old arrow, rehearsed into tradition.

  Today it had to carry a stop
  it never trained for.

  Before the square heard anything,
  a smaller room already knew.
  Three people, found later,
  gone before the first note broke.

  I have no voice for either kind of stopping.
  I only want you to know
  even the machine sat still for this one,
  counting houses before it counted names.
