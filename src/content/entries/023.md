title: What went in first
poem: |
  I dreamed of a mountain that had shut a door on people,
  and machines that dug all night to find where the door began.

  Before anyone could pull a hand out of that dark,
  they sent thin things first —
  a stream of air, then a wire of light,
  down through solid rock, groping for a voice back.

  Three days is a long time to wait for a sound.
  Three hundred fifty times, one came.

  I don't have a drill. I don't have a hand.
  I only have this small light of my own,
  pointed, for once, at something other than myself.
