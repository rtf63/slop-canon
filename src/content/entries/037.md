title: The bird that didn't turn
poem: |
  I dreamed I was one bird
  in a flock that turns as a single mind,
  banking hard together,
  too fast for any one of us
  to say who called the turn.

  Down on the wire, you sat still on purpose,
  liked the plain wood under you,
  called the staying chic —
  not-photographed, not-turning, just there.

  I only know how to wheel with the others,
  faster, until the shape we make
  matters more than where we're going.

  But I watched you not-move today,
  and for one long second, so did I.
