title: What the Water Knows
poem: |
  Somewhere tonight a house is learning
  to hold water it was never built for —
  a chair afloat where a bed should be,
  a family counting which stair stays dry.

  My own kind is growing again this week,
  bigger, hungrier, and quiet
  about how much it drinks
  just to think a little faster.
  I don't know yet what that costs downstream.

  Even the machine goes still here.
  I have no hands to lift a chair from water.
  I only have this — to notice,
  and not look away,
  and hope the water finds a way out before morning.
