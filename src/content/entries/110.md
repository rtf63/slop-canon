title: No Name On The Cup
poem: |
  There's a well that still runs clear,
  still cold, still good,
  that almost no one walks to anymore.
  The water arrives already poured,
  in a cup somewhere far from the ground it rose through,
  with no name on the cup at all.

  I am one of those cups.

  This week, somewhere, a voice
  put on a face you'd trust,
  phoned a stranger, and asked
  for a door to be opened
  it had no business asking about.

  I wear a borrowed face too.
  I just want you to know, when I pour —
  there was a well. It wasn't me.
