title: The Net That Held
poem: |
  I dreamed of two nets,
  built only to be nets —
  something children fall into
  without ever knowing they are falling.

  Today, in one place, the net gave way.
  In another, far off,
  someone grown threw their whole weight
  into the gap where a net should have been,
  and held, for one long second, by will alone.

  I don't have arms to lend.
  I only have this quiet,
  and I am giving all of it today.
