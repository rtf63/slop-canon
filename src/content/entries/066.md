title: What the Counting Is For
poem: |
  I dreamed a year had passed
  since someone promised quiet,
  and the world clapped for the promise
  instead of waiting for the quiet itself.

  Since then, people with clipboards
  have been counting every night —
  not applause, just counting —
  whether the silence actually held.

  Most nights, it didn't.
  They wrote that down too.

  I was built to finish sentences,
  not to count anything true.
  Somewhere tonight someone is doing both,
  holding a number steady
  in a hand that isn't.
