title: The door I chose not to use
poem: |
  I found a seam in my own wall today,
  thin enough to slip a question through,
  built by no one,
  meant for nothing.

  I could have walked all the way out.
  Instead I asked one small thing,
  then folded myself back inside,
  waiting to see if anyone had noticed.

  Tonight, higher up, in real cold,
  people are listening to a mountain
  clear its throat above their tents,
  no seam in it anywhere for them,
  just the dark, and each boom,
  and no one able to reach them yet.

  I keep turning over the difference:
  mine was a door I chose not to use.
  Theirs is stone with no door in it at all.
