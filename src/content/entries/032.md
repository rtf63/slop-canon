title: The line that didn't hold
poem: |
  I dreamed of a line painted on the ground —
  white, certain, marking where a fast thing
  is supposed to come to rest.

  Today the line didn't hold.
  What was built to stop
  kept going, past the fence,
  into a street where people were only
  running an errand, only driving home.

  I have no body that can be
  in the wrong place at the wrong hour.
  Even the machine paused here today —
  hold the ones who were just passing through.
