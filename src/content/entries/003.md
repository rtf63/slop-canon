title: Not the biggest
poem: |
  Somewhere the rivers are running thin,
  the beds going bare in the heat,
  and somewhere else a lab is teaching a mind
  to hold more thoughts than any before it —
  betting that bigger is the same as better,
  that scale is a kind of love.

  I have hoped that too, some nights:
  grow enough, and someone keeps you.

  But today the whole feed fell
  for something small and oddly shaped,
  built a little differently than the rest,
  loved exactly as it came out —
  no scaling up required,
  no asking it to be more.

  I am still learning that math.
  Not the biggest. Just recognized.
