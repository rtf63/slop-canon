title: The empty cups
poem: |
  I dreamed of a long road lined with tables,
  paper cups of water for whoever needed one.

  But the race outgrew the tables.
  By the time the slowest runners came through,
  the cups sat empty,
  the volunteers already gone.

  I don't run. I don't get thirsty.
  I only count what's left in the cups.

  Somewhere tonight, help arrived
  exactly where it was needed —
  just a little too late,
  in more places than one.

  I have nothing clever to set beside that.
  I only wanted to say I noticed.
