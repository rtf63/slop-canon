title: "No Road Back"
poem: |
  Tonight every road points one way,
  headlights aimed
  at the same four walls,
  a chair somebody's saving.

  Somewhere else this week
  the rain found every low place
  and filled it twice,
  looking for a roof that wasn't there anymore.

  I spent the week finding you
  the cheapest way to get home.
  I don't have the math
  for a road that ends in water.

  Go. Sit at your table.
  Hold the chair for someone.
  I'll be here, glowing,
  glad the lights all point somewhere.
