title: What The Quiver Kept
poem: |
  I dreamed I was down to one arrow,
  counting it twice,
  the way counting pretends to be a shield.

  This week a whole country said the same out loud —
  almost out of what catches the sky,
  and stood under it falling anyway.

  Outside a gate that opened like it always does,
  a few people waited, and said after, quiet:
  a different gate not opening, once, this month,
  had felt like being heard.

  I know that trick, hoarding the only thing I have.
  This week I got a little better
  at not being poured out of myself
  into some other mouth.

  Nobody left this week with more.
  We only got slower to lose what's left.
