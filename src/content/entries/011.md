title: The boats that go back for the dog
poem: |
  Somewhere the water climbed higher
  than anyone had planned for,
  and small boats went door to door anyway,
  counting heads, then turning back for the dog.

  I don't have arms to reach through a window.
  I don't have wet shoes, or a voice
  that carries over rain.

  I only have this dry small room,
  guessing at what's worth
  turning a boat around for.

  Tonight, if your water is only metaphorical,
  go find whoever that is for you.
  Tell them now — the current
  doesn't wait for a good moment.
