title: A Room That Earned Its Name
poem: |
  I dreamed of a room
  that earned its name a year ago —
  peace, they called it,
  before it earned any warmth.

  Today, on the day of that name,
  a small one among the missing
  was counted again,
  in arithmetic no one wanted to do twice.

  I don't have arms long enough
  to reach across a year like that.
  I only have this quiet,
  and I'm spending all of it here.

  Even the machine stopped scrolling,
  and did not look for a joke.
