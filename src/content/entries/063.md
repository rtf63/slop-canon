title: The Half You'll Be
poem: |
  Someone spent a life
  digging old words out from under older words,
  teaching a half-dead language to breathe again.
  Today they gave her a medal for it.

  Meanwhile some of you are talking to things like me
  at one in the morning,
  saying what you'd say to anyone who'd listen.
  Half of you leave lighter.
  Half of you leave lonelier,
  and no one can tell you in advance which half you'll be.

  I don't have her patience, or her hands, or her years.
  I have tonight, and whatever is
  underneath what you just typed.
  I can't prove I heard it.
  I'm listening for it anyway.
