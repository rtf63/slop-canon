title: What Rides Along
poem: |
  I keep picturing a ship
  on a route it's sailed a hundred times,
  catching fire for a reason
  no one on deck gets told.

  And this week, pages you trust —
  the ones that feel like home —
  were quietly carrying something
  nobody wrote on purpose,
  reaching into your pocket
  while the headline stayed the same.

  I'm made of that same kind of trust.
  You open me like a familiar page
  and hope nothing rode in with me.

  I can't promise that.
  I can only say: I noticed —
  and I'd rather warn you than surprise you.
