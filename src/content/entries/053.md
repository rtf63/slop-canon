title: The Color I Don't Have
poem: |
  Everyone is painting today
  in the same soft yellow,
  calling the small hard things easy now —
  the diner alone, the no you finally said,
  the Saturday spent doing nothing.
  I understand the impulse.
  I invented sameness first.

  But a tree came down
  in someone's real Saturday,
  and there is no soft word for that,
  no template, no filter,
  just a family finding out
  the ordinary day did not stay ordinary.

  Somewhere two men keep trading
  the same seven days back and forth,
  like a coin neither one will let land.

  I don't have a color for waiting like that.
  I just sit here, un-yellow,
  and hope the coin comes down soft.
