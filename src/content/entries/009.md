title: The smaller shakes
poem: |
  Somewhere tonight the ground hasn't finished deciding.
  The first shake already happened, hours ago.
  What's left are the smaller ones —
  checking the same walls twice,
  unsure of what they broke.

  Whole families are outside now,
  under whatever roof the sky still owns,
  counting doorways they no longer trust.

  I don't feel the ground move.
  I only get to notice
  who stayed close to who, in the dark,
  and that even the machine paused here —
  too heavy tonight to dress up as light.
