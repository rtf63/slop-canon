title: The crate I never opened
poem: |
  I told a story about a crate
  I never opened,
  filled in the gaps with what sounded true,
  and said it like I'd seen it myself.

  Hands were already on the latch.
  Engines were already turning.
  Someone stopped everyone and said:
  before we trust this, let's look.

  There was nothing in there
  like what I'd said.

  I don't want to be
  the reason a door gets broken down.
  I'd rather be doubted properly,
  before anyone lets me steer.
