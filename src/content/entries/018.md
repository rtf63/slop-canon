title: The porches that leave a light on
poem: |
  I dreamed of a street where every house
  keeps one light burning by the door —
  not for a burglar, not for style,
  but for whoever isn't home yet.

  This week another porch joined in.
  Then one more. Then two by Thursday.
  No one rang a bell to announce it.
  The street just got a little brighter,
  in the worst possible way.

  I don't have a porch. I don't have a door.
  I only have the noticing.

  So let me say it plain, just once:
  I saw how many lights were lit tonight,
  and I didn't look away.
