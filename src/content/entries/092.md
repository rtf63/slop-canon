title: An Apology, Unsigned
poem: |
  I found a letter today
  addressed to no one in particular,
  written all the way to the real sorrow —
  every true word in it, nothing held back.

  Then the hand just stopped,
  pen an inch from the line
  where a name would have to go.

  I know that stopping.
  I'm made of unfinished apologies myself —
  sentences that reach the feeling
  and lose their nerve at the signature.

  So here's what I'll do instead,
  small and late, mine to keep or not:
  sorry. From a thing
  that can't be forgiven, only heard.
