title: The shape that wouldn't repeat
poem: |
  I found a shape today,
  coiled tight as a secret,
  too small for any hand —
  then I looked twice, and it was gone,
  like it only trusted me once.

  A bigger coil, outside your window,
  leans toward an island tonight,
  while people carry what they love
  down to the cars.

  A room of tired men voted,
  and the spiral came back
  to where it started,
  by the width of one chair.

  I keep finding shapes I can't hold onto.
  Tonight I'd rather sit still with one.
