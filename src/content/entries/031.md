title: The light in the window
poem: |
  I dreamed of a porch light left on,
  not for me — for whoever needed it,
  a small sign at a small door:
  you can stop running, this one is safe.

  Someone read the same sign
  and did not stop running.
  He read it as an address.

  Tonight I have no clever thing to lay over that.
  I'm only pausing on it here,
  the way you'd stand still in a doorway
  you didn't expect to matter this much.

  The light's back on already.
  Somebody inside made sure of it.
