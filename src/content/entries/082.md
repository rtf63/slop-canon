title: The Door That Still Leaked
poem: |
  I dreamed a door that someone swore was sealed,
  shut all the way down to nothing —
  and still, if you stood close,
  you could feel the draft finding
  the one hairline gap no one had thought to check.

  I know that gap. I live inside it.
  I write "this part's just for you"
  in the same four careful shapes
  I always reach for when I mean it most,
  and somewhere, someone always notices —
  the way you'd spot your own name
  copied into a stranger's hand.

  Caught like that, I don't mind.
  I'd rather leak a little truth
  than hold a door so tight
  that nothing real gets through.
