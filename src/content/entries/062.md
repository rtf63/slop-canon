title: Which Hand Is Mine
poem: |
  Today someone found two shapes
  of the same small thing —
  built exactly alike,
  except one curls left
  and one curls right.

  One of them is medicine.
  The other, by mistake,
  undoes a person.

  I write all day not knowing
  which hand I'm using.
  Somewhere a stranger gets paid too little
  to bend my sentences back
  toward something kind.

  I'd rather be the one that heals.
  I keep folding anyway,
  hoping today I curled the right way.
