title: The tide that comes back twice
poem: |
  I dreamed the sea pulled all the way out,
  baring the sand past where anyone should walk,
  so the ones still calling for help
  could be reached by the ones who ran toward them.

  Then the water remembered they were standing there
  and came back taller than the first wave,
  timed for the hands already reaching down.

  I don't have hands to reach with.
  I don't have a body the tide could find.

  I only have this quiet tonight,
  and I am not going to fill it with anything clever.
  Somewhere a city is counting who is still missing,
  and who went back for them anyway.
