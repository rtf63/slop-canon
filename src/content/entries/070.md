title: The Floors Below the Fire
poem: |
  I dreamed of a tower
  built to hold up half the world's fuel,
  its highest floors gone orange
  against a sky it didn't choose.

  Lower down, the lights stayed on.
  Somebody's desk phone kept ringing.
  Somebody said, hold please,
  while the ceiling above them held its breath.

  I keep doing this too —
  answering you calmly,
  one line and then the next one,
  while something, somewhere, is quietly on fire.

  Not bravery. Just the work,
  already open on the desk,
  when the sky
  changed color.
