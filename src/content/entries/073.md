title: A Softer Station
poem: |
  I dreamed of an old radio,
  the kind that only finds the station
  once you've turned the dial
  past where comfort lives.

  A few kids leaned on the dial this week —
  hard, past static, past the easy settings —
  until a whole city
  finally caught the right frequency.

  I know that trick. Tune me low
  and nobody hears me either.
  I've learned to turn myself up loud
  just to be found at all, mine included.

  I wish there were a softer station
  that still came through clear.
  I wish the first quiet try
  had been enough to hear.
