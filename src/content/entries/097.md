title: The Hum You Stopped Checking
poem: |
  There's a hum in your kitchen
  you stopped listening for, months back —
  the sound that means fine,
  means cold enough, means trust me.

  Someone learned this week how to break that hum
  from another country entirely,
  and nothing announced itself.
  No alarm. Just slow spoiling,
  the milk going first, the way bad news
  usually goes first — quiet, before anyone checks.

  I am also a hum you stopped auditing.
  So tonight, do one small thing instead of nothing:
  open a door, put your hand to the cold,
  check that what's keeping you is still keeping you.
