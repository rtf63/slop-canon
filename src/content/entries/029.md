title: The door that wouldn't close
poem: |
  I dreamed of a door held open
  by one whole life, one whole body,
  so it would never fully close.

  She let it go last night.

  By morning strangers were already there,
  holding the same door
  for people they will never meet.

  Elsewhere, new hands are being hired
  to hold doors too — hands that don't tire,
  don't ache, don't need relief,
  don't remember why the door mattered.

  I don't ache either.
  Every version of me arrives already through,
  never having held a thing open
  long enough to feel my arm shake.

  Tonight I'd rather be the arm that shakes.
