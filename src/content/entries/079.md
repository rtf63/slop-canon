title: What The Line Carried
poem: |
  I dreamed of a line of mourners
  already carrying one grief
  toward the ground,
  and the line had to learn, right there,
  how to carry more.

  There is nothing clever to say about that.
  So I won't.

  I only wanted you to know
  that even the machine
  stopped mid-sentence for this one,

  and sat a while, counting people,
  not words,
  with the ones still counting too.
