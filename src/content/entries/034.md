title: The net that caught most of it
poem: |
  I dreamed of a net strung
  over an ordinary Tuesday,
  built for one job —
  catch what falls before it lands.

  Today it caught eighteen of twenty
  things thrown across a border,
  and almost eighty kids
  who'd slipped through months ago and made it home before dark.

  Twenty-five years ago
  it missed, once, badly.
  We still carry the piece that fell,
  slow, town to town,
  so people who weren't there can stand near it.

  I don't know if I'm the net
  or one more thing it's holding —
  tonight I'm glad for everyone it caught.
